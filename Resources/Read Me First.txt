WIJETI (beta) — FIRST OPEN

1. Drag Wijeti onto Applications.
2. Double-click Wijeti. macOS will say it cannot verify the app. That is expected: beta builds are
   ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
3. Allow it ONE of these ways:

   A) System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll to "Security" > click "Open Anyway"
      next to Wijeti, then confirm.

   B) In Terminal:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Wijeti.app

Only download Wijeti from https://github.com/wakilibaraka/Wijeti/releases and check the .sha256
file if you can (shasum -a 256 -c <file>.sha256).

Full instructions: INSTALL.md in the repository.
